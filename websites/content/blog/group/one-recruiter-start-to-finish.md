In many agencies, the person who wins your business is not the person who works on it. Your brief gets passed to someone else, details get lost and you end up explaining everything twice.

## For employers

You brief the Head of Recruitment directly. The person who understands your role is the same person screening candidates, negotiating the offer and checking in after the start date.

## For candidates

You speak to someone who knows the employer, the team and the real reasons a role is open. You get honest feedback, not silence.

## How we keep it fast

Personal accountability does not mean slow. We combine it with modern sourcing and outreach tools, so we can search widely and contact the right people quickly, while keeping every conversation personal.
