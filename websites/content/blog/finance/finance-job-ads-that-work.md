Most finance job ads read the same: a list of duties, "competitive salary" and "fast-paced environment". Strong candidates scroll straight past.

## Lead with the salary

Put a real range in the ad. Ads without a salary get fewer applications, and the people who do apply often have very different expectations to yours.

## Be specific about the work

Say which systems you use, what the month-end looks like, the size of the team and who the person reports to. "Prepare management accounts for three entities in Xero and report to the FD" says more than a page of generic duties.

## Say how you work

Hybrid? How many days in the office? Flexible start times? Study support? These are often the deciding factors for candidates.

## Keep the must-haves short

List the few things that are truly essential. Long wish lists put off capable people, especially women and career changers, who are less likely to apply unless they tick every box.

## Make it easy to apply

A short application process with a quick response wins candidates. If it takes three weeks to hear back, your best applicants will already be in interviews elsewhere.

> Better still, let us write the ad and approach suitable people directly. Most strong candidates are not reading job boards at all.
