If you are starting out in finance, choosing a qualification is one of the biggest career decisions you will make. Here is how the three main routes tend to be seen by the employers we work with.

## ACA (ICAEW)

Traditionally linked with practice and audit, often trained within accountancy firms. Widely respected, and a common route into financial reporting, technical accounting and, later, finance director roles.

## ACCA

Flexible and international, with a broad syllabus. It suits people working in industry or practice who want options. Very common in financial accounting and controller roles.

## CIMA

Focused on management accounting and business partnering. Popular in industry, especially for people who want to move into commercial finance, FP&A and business partner roles.

## What employers actually care about

- **Progress.** Are you passing exams steadily?
- **Experience.** What you have actually done often matters more than the letters.
- **Fit.** Practice, industry and public sector have different working styles.

## Our advice

Pick the route that matches the work you enjoy, then find an employer who supports your study with paid leave and fees. We prioritise those employers for our part-qualified candidates.
