A good rate on paper can turn into a bad week if the details are not right. Before you say yes, get clear answers to these five questions.

## 1. How am I being paid?

PAYE through the agency, umbrella company or your own limited company all mean different take-home pay. Ask what the rate is and how it is paid, then work out what actually lands in your account.

## 2. What are the hours, really?

Is it eight hours or ten? Are Saturdays expected? Is overtime paid at a higher rate? A day rate means nothing without the hours.

## 3. How long is the job?

Two weeks or six months makes a big difference to whether it is worth the travel. Ask about the chance of extension too.

## 4. Where is it and how do I get there?

Check parking, travel time and whether any travel or accommodation is paid for jobs further afield.

## 5. When do I get paid?

Weekly, and on which day? Who do you call if a timesheet goes missing?
