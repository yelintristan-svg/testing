Construction is lumpy. Groundworks need different people from fit-out, and a pipeline of three projects rarely lines up neatly. The firms that stay profitable treat workforce planning as part of the programme.

## Keep a permanent core

Your site managers, supervisors, QSs and key trades carry your standards and your client relationships. These are the people worth hiring permanently and keeping.

## Flex the rest by phase

- **Enabling and groundworks.** Groundworkers, plant operators and labourers, often for a few weeks.
- **Structure and envelope.** Larger crews of skilled trades, steel fixers, carpenters and scaffolders.
- **M&E and fit-out.** Electricians, plumbers, dryliners and decorators, ramping up as you close in.
- **Snagging and handover.** A small multi-skilled team.

## Plan your labour like your materials

Share your programme with your recruiter at the start, not when the gap appears. We can line up the right trades for each phase, check tickets in advance and have people ready for the start date.

## Watch for the hidden costs

Late bookings, unvetted labour and constant churn all cost money in rework, delays and site safety. A reliable supply of the same faces from phase to phase is worth more than the cheapest day rate.

> Permanent for the people who define your business. Contract for the work that comes and goes.
