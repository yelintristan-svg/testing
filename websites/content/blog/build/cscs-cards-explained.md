No card, no site. Most main contractors will not let you through the gate without a valid CSCS card, so it is worth knowing which one fits your role.

## The main cards

- **Green (Labourer).** For general labouring roles. You will need the Level 1 Award in Health and Safety in a Construction Environment and the CITB Health, Safety and Environment test.
- **Blue (Skilled Worker).** For qualified tradespeople with an NVQ or SVQ Level 2, or a completed apprenticeship.
- **Gold (Advanced Craft or Supervisor).** For NVQ Level 3 tradespeople and site supervisors.
- **Black (Manager).** For site, project and contracts managers with a relevant Level 5 or above qualification.
- **Red (Trainee, Apprentice and others).** Temporary cards while you work towards a qualification.
- **White (Professionally or Academically Qualified).** For roles such as engineers and surveyors with recognised qualifications or professional body membership.

## Before you book anything

Card requirements change over time, so always check the official CSCS card finder for your exact occupation before paying for courses or tests.

## Other tickets that help you get work

CPCS or NPORS for plant operators, SSSTS for supervisors, SMSTS for site managers, IPAF and PASMA for access equipment, and 18th Edition for electricians. The more current tickets you hold, the more jobs we can put you forward for.
