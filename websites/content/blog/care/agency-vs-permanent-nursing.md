Plenty of nurses and carers move between agency and permanent work during their careers. Neither is better. It depends on what you need right now.

## Why people choose agency work

- **Flexibility.** You choose your shifts and can take time off when you want it.
- **Variety.** Different settings build experience fast, from care homes to acute wards.
- **Pay.** Hourly rates are usually higher than the equivalent permanent band.

## Why people choose permanent roles

- **Security.** Guaranteed hours and income every month.
- **Benefits.** Pension contributions, sick pay and paid study time.
- **Progression.** It is easier to step up to senior or management roles inside one organisation.

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Do I need a fixed monthly income for a mortgage or rent application?
2. How much do I value choosing my own hours?
3. Am I working towards a specialism or promotion?
4. Would I like to try a few places before committing to one?

## You don't have to pick forever

Many of our nurses start on agency shifts, find a home or ward they love, and then move into a permanent role there. Others go the other way after years on a rota. Tell us what you want and we will find the right mix.
