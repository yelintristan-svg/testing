The single biggest delay between registering with an agency and starting work is paperwork. Get it together in one go and you could be on shift within days.

## Identity and right to work

- Passport or biometric residence permit
- Share code if you are on a visa
- Proof of address from the last three months
- National Insurance number

## Checks and registration

- Enhanced DBS (or your update service certificate)
- NMC PIN for registered nurses, HCPC for allied health professionals
- Two references, ideally including your most recent employer

## Training

Most settings expect up-to-date mandatory training, including moving and handling, basic life support, safeguarding adults and children, infection control, fire safety and medication awareness where relevant. If anything has lapsed, we can point you to approved courses.

## Health

Occupational health clearance and immunisation records, such as Hepatitis B, are often needed for clinical settings.

## Make it easy on yourself

Scan everything once and keep it in one folder. When you register with us, we will tell you exactly which items are missing, rather than leaving you to guess.
