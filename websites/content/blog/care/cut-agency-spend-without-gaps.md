---
title: How to cut agency spend without leaving shifts uncovered
date: 2026-10-14
audience: client
excerpt: Agency costs creep up when cover is booked in a panic. A few changes to how you plan shifts can bring the bill down without risking safe staffing.
---
Most care providers don't overspend on agency staff because they use agencies. They overspend because cover gets booked late, at premium rates, with whoever is free.

## Book the predictable gaps early

Annual leave, training days and known vacancies are not emergencies. Share your four-week rota gaps with your agency as soon as you know them. Block bookings are cheaper than last-minute shifts and you get the same faces back.

## Ask for a consistent pool

Residents and patients do better with familiar staff, and so does your permanent team. Ask your agency to build a small, named pool of nurses and carers who already know your home or ward. Fewer inductions, fewer mistakes.

## Know your true cost per shift

Compare the all-in agency rate against the real cost of overtime, sickness and burnout among your own staff. Sometimes a planned agency shift is the cheaper option. Sometimes it is a sign you need another permanent hire.

## Convert your best temps

If a temporary carer or nurse is already doing great work on your shifts, a temp-to-perm move is often the lowest-risk hire you will ever make. Agree the terms up front so it is simple when the moment comes.

## Check compliance once, properly

Every agency worker should arrive with DBS, right to work, registration and mandatory training already verified. If you are re-checking paperwork on the morning of the shift, your agency is not doing its job.

> The aim is not zero agency use. It is planned, predictable cover at a fair rate.
